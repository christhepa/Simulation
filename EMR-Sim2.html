    About this tool

    This is a self-contained training EMR simulator built for PA program small-group and case-based instruction. Its purpose is to give students hands-on practice with the everyday mechanics of electronic charting — reviewing a patient list, opening a chart, placing orders, reviewing results, and writing standard note types. The interface layout and navigation (storyboard sidebar, activity tabs, chart flow) are modeled on conventions common to commercial EMRs, including Epic, to build workflow familiarity that transfers to clinical rotations. This tool is independently built for educational use and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a certified replica of Epic Systems Corporation or any other EMR vendor.

    Included in this simulation:

    • A panel of fictional patients, each with a self-contained case (history, baseline vitals, and a pre-written admission History & Physical to review)
    • Order entry for common labs, imaging studies, bedside/invasive procedures, and medications — including a free-text custom medication order and a basic teaching-oriented allergy-alert check — with a simulated pending → resulted/active lifecycle
    • Documentation tools for Progress Notes, History & Physical notes, Consultations, and Discharge Summaries
    • An "+ Add Patient" tool for instructors or students to author additional cases, either through a guided form or by importing a JSON case file
    • A per-patient "Facilitator Panel" for pre-authoring the intended lab results of a progressively unfolding case across multiple stages, and revealing each stage on cue

    All data is stored only in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server, and nothing here should ever be substituted for a real medical record system or used with real patient information. Use Reset course data (top right) to wipe all student-entered notes and orders and restore every chart to its original seeded state.
